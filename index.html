Methodology and limitations
Area selection: Administrative and enumeration-area boundaries are obtained from the configured public JSON file. Statistics are calculated from the selected boundary features and their available attributes.

Satellite imagery: Sentinel-2 imagery is displayed through the Esri Sentinel-2 ImageServer and its available raster functions.

Important: The map's satellite rendering is not itself a zonal statistical calculation. NDVI, NDMI, NDWI and NDBI map renderings should not be interpreted as measured mean index values or class areas. Sentinel-2 does not contain thermal infrared bands suitable for land surface temperature retrieval. Actual LST analysis requires a thermal source such as Landsat or MODIS.
