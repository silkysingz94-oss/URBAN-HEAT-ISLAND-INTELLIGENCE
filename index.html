Methodology and limitations

Numeric statistics are calculated from point samples returned by the Sentinel-2 ImageServer Identify operation using the selected raster function. Samples are restricted to points inside the selected boundary features.

The charts represent sample values, not complete pixel-by-pixel zonal statistics. Missing or cloudy imagery can result in NoData responses.

NDVI measures vegetation response; NDBI is a built-up spectral indicator; NDWI is a water-related indicator; NBR is a burn-related indicator. These are not direct surface-temperature measurements.

Sentinel-2 has no thermal bands. Land Surface Temperature analysis requires thermal imagery such as Landsat or MODIS LST.
